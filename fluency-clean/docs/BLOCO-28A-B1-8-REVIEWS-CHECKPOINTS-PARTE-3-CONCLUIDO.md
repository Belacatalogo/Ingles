## Detalhamento das aulas

### B1-GRAMMAR-021 — Relative clauses

**Objetivos:** Defining (who/which/that/where/whose) vs. non-defining (com vírgulas, sem "that")

**Conteúdo pedagógico:**
- Tabela com 5 pronomes relativos + defining/non-defining + notas
- whenToUse: 4 itens; whenNotToUse: 4 itens (that em non-defining, vírgulas, what, pronome duplo)
- 6 teacherExamples (defining, non-defining, whose, where)
- 4 commonBrazilianMistakes (what, that em non-defining, pronome duplo, whose her)
- 3 controlledPractice sets, 1 errorCorrectionPractice (4 frases), 3 translationPractice
- 2 productionTasks (describe someone, transform from checkpoint essays)

### B1-GRAMMAR-022 — Question tags e indirect questions

**Objetivos:** Formação de tags (positive→negative, auxiliary matching), indirect questions (word order)

**Conteúdo pedagógico:**
- Tabela com 7 exemplos (presente simples, passado, PPC, modal, "I am")
- whenToUse: 4 itens; whenNotToUse: 3 itens
- 6 teacherExamples em contextos conversacionais reais
- 4 commonBrazilianMistakes (isn't it, where is, positive→positive, does she live)
- 2 controlledPractice sets (6 tags + 4 indirect), 1 errorCorrectionPractice (4 frases), 3 translationPractice
- 2 productionTasks (conversation practice, polite enquiry roleplay)

### B1-VOCABULARY-017 — Word-building

**12 itens:** prefixos (un-, dis-, re-, over-, mis-, pre-) e sufixos (-ful, -less, -ness, -tion/-sion, -ment, -er/-or)

**6 chunks:** make an improvement, reach an agreement, lack of awareness, lead to a misunderstanding, be an achievement, raise awareness of

**3 dangerousConfusions:** sensible vs sensitive, informations/advices/knowledges (uncountable), overworked vs overwhelm

**1 mini-diálogo:** 6 turnos — Carla e Dan discutem sobrecarga de trabalho usando 10 palavras construídas com os padrões da aula

**2 productionTasks:** word-building paragraph (80–100 palavras, 6+ formas construídas), rapid-fire word families

### B1-SPEAKING-009 — B1 completion speaking

**Modelo:** 180 palavras — narrativa de experiência transformadora (mudança para o exterior) com non-defining relative clause, 3rd conditional, mixed conditional, past perfect continuous, "looking back"

**10 modelPhrases** cobrindo: narrative openers, relative clauses em narrativa, condicionais reflexivos, PPC, insights

**3 estágios:** escolha da experiência → estrutura → entrega 2-3 minutos

**6 B1 markers anotados** no modelo

**speakingChecklist:** 6 itens incluindo relative clause, conditional e 2+ minutos sem script

### B1-WRITING-009 — B1 completion writing (formal letter)

**Modelo:** carta formal completa de enquiry sobre curso de inglês — Dear Sir/Madam, salutation, indirect questions, PPC, formal vocabulary, Yours faithfully

**8 anotações de breakdown:** salutation, reason for writing, background (PPC), indirect questions, closing, faithful/sincerely, register, grammar integration

**4 commonWritingMistakes:** "Dear John", faithful vs sincerely, direct vs indirect questions, contrações em formal

**draftTask:** 3 opções (enquiry, application, complaint) com 150–180 palavras

**revisionChecklist:** 10 itens
