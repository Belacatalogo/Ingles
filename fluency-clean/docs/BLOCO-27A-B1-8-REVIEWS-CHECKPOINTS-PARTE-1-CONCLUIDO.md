## Detalhamento das aulas

### B1-GRAMMAR-019 — Mixed conditionals

**Objetivos:** Combinar 2nd+3rd conditional — passado→presente e presente→passado

**Conteúdo pedagógico:**
- Tabela com 4 linhas: mixed (past→present), mixed (present→past), 2nd conditional (reminder), 3rd conditional (reminder)
- whenToUse: 4 itens; whenNotToUse: 3 itens (incluindo "would" in if-clause, random mixing, was vs were)
- 6 teacherExamples em contextos de carreira, linguagem, tecnologia, personalidade
- 4 commonBrazilianMistakes (would have been, was→were, past participle, false 3rd)
- 4 controlledPractice, 1 errorCorrectionPractice, 3 translationPractice
- 2 productionTasks (life reflection, partner discussion)

### B1-GRAMMAR-020 — Present Perfect Continuous

**Objetivos:** "have/has been + -ing" para duração ongoing, estado atual explicado por atividade recente, contraste com Simple

**Conteúdo pedagógico:**
- Tabela com 4 formas (affirmative, negative, question + stative reminder)
- whenToUse: 4 itens; whenNotToUse: 3 itens (stative verbs, completed results/numbers, permanent states)
- 6 teacherExamples: learning duration, explaining tired face, recent crying, waiting, social media, work project
- 4 commonBrazilianMistakes (am studying since, been knowing, two articles→Simple, been finished)
- 3 controlledPractice sets, 1 errorCorrectionPractice, 3 translationPractice
- 2 productionTasks (language learning journey, evidence explanation)

### B1-VOCABULARY-015 — B1 vocabulary review

**12 palavras integradoras:** overcome, perspective, contribute, relevant, assumption, consequence, efficient, broadcast, solidarity, ambiguous, adjust, worthwhile

**6 chunks integradores:** make progress (in/with), take something for granted, come to terms with, keep an open mind, on the one hand… on the other hand, have a positive/negative impact on

**3 dangerousConfusions:** affect vs effect, despite/in spite of vs although, raise vs rise

**1 mini-diálogo:** 7 turnos — Ana e Tom discutem media bias, fake news e linguagem B1 integrada (inclui mixed conditional e PPC)

**2 productionTasks:** review paragraph (100–130 palavras com 5 vocabulários obrigatórios), error correction challenge (3 itens)

### B1-SPEAKING-008 — B1 speaking checkpoint

**Modelo:** 165 palavras — resposta estruturada 2 minutos sobre tecnologia e felicidade, integrando mixed conditional, PPC, discourse markers B1

**12 modelPhrases** cobrindo todo o espectro B1: "To my mind", "On the one hand", "I've been thinking", "If I had grown up", "On balance, I would argue"

**3 estágios:** preparação (2 min) → entrega (2 min) → reflexão (5 min)

**B1 markers anotados:** 6 features linguísticas destacadas no modelo

**speakingChecklist:** 7 itens de autoavaliação

### B1-WRITING-008 — B1 writing checkpoint

**Modelo:** 4 parágrafos completos "Is technology making our lives better or worse?" — integrated mixed conditional, PPC, passive voice, 4 discourse markers, B1.7/B1.8 vocabulary

**8 anotações de breakdown** explicando cada elemento retórico

**4 commonWritingMistakes:** listas, afirmações absolutas, conclusão vazia, falta de concession

**4 writingBlocks:** scaffolded paragraph structure (intro, main argument, concession, conclusion)

**revisionChecklist:** 10 itens + draft/revision/finalVersionTask
