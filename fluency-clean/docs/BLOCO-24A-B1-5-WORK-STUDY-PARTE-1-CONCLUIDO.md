## Aulas criadas neste bloco

### Grammar

- `B1-GRAMMAR-012` — Passive voice: present and past simple

Foco:
- is/are + pp (presente), was/were + pp (passado);
- quando usar: agente desconhecido, óbvio ou irrelevante;
- "by" para agente quando necessário;
- tabela: tempo / forma ativa / forma passiva / exemplo;
- erros: "wrote" (wrong pp), passado com "is", "by nobody";
- 4 commonBrazilianMistakes, 2 controlledPractice, error correction, translation, 2 productionTasks.

- `B1-GRAMMAR-013` — Reported speech: statements

Foco:
- backshift completo: present simple → past, will → would, can → could, present perfect → past perfect;
- mudança de pronomes e expressões de tempo (tomorrow → the next day);
- "said" vs "told": said = sem objeto de pessoa; told = com objeto de pessoa;
- erros: "said me", "will" sem backshift, "sended";
- tabela de backshift com 6 exemplos, 2 controlledPractice, error correction, translation, 2 productionTasks.

### Vocabulary

- `B1-VOCABULARY-009` — Work and professional vocabulary

Foco:
- 12 palavras: line manager, colleague, take on, hand in, chase up, fall behind, meet a deadline, delegate, exceed expectations, be on track, notice period, probation period;
- 6 chunks: responsible for, report to, currently working on, behind schedule, follow up on;
- 3 dangerousConfusions: resign vs retire, "work since" (wrong), make/do + collocations;
- 1 miniDialogues (Ana + Interviewer, 6 linhas);
- 2 productionTasks.

### Speaking

- `B1-SPEAKING-005` — Describe your job or studies

Foco:
- situação: networking event ("So, what do you do?");
- modelResponse ~120 palavras (cargo, responsabilidades, equipe, projeto atual, reflexão pessoal);
- 12 modelPhrases profissionais;
- 2 substitutionDrills (responsible for, currently working on);
- 3 guidedSpeaking tasks;
- 6 speakingChecklist;
- 1 recordingTask: 90-120s;
- 3 freeSpeaking prompts.

### Writing

- `B1-WRITING-005` — Write a professional email

Foco:
- modelText: follow-up email (Ana → Mr Costa, 140 palavras);
- modelTextBreakdown: 10 anotações (subject, greeting, abertura, propósito, "as discussed", flag problema, pedido de confirmação, segundo pedido, encerramento, assinatura);
- 4 commonWritingMistakes (Waiting for your answer, "writing to you about", "I want", Best sem assinatura);
- 8 revisionChecklist;
- draftTask, revisionTask, finalVersionTask.
