### Grammar

- `B1-GRAMMAR-011` — Third Conditional: If I had known, I would have...

Foco:
- estrutura: if + past perfect / would have + past participle;
- situações irreais no PASSADO — contraste com Second Conditional (presente/futuro);
- "If I would have..." — o erro mais grave do B1 brasileiro;
- "had had" — dois "had": auxiliar + verbo principal;
- tabela completa com if-clause, main clause, negativas, pergunta, estrutura mista;
- 4 commonBrazilianMistakes, 2 controlledPractice, error correction, translation, 2 productionTasks.

### Reading

- `B1-READING-004` — The career dilemma: risk or safety?

Foco:
- texto ~280 palavras: dilema entre segurança e oportunidade;
- 4 parágrafos: situação → medo → explicação psicológica → conclusão;
- vocabulário: weigh up, paralysis, leap of faith, regret inaction;
- 5 comprehensionQuestions com evidence e why;
- 3 vocabulary tasks (inferência);
- guidedSummary com modelo de 4 frases;
- productionTask: posição pessoal com vocabulário do texto.

### Listening

- `B1-LISTENING-004` — Should I take the risk? (Sofia + Marcus)

Foco:
- diálogo Sofia + Marcus sobre dilema de cargo sênior (16 linhas);
- Second Conditional ("If I take it..."), Third Conditional ("If I had been offered..."), 3 modais de conselho (should, ought to, had better);
- 5 comprehensionQuestions incluindo análise de estrutura gramatical;
- 3 vocabulary tasks (regret inaction, go for it, on balance);
- 3 shadowing: "If I were you, I would...", "ought to", "had better";
- oralProduction: 60-90s como Marcus.

### Vocabulary

- `B1-VOCABULARY-008` — Decision-making vocabulary

Foco:

- 6 chunks: going back and forth, on balance, the way I see it, calculated risk, at the end of the day;
- 3 dangerousConfusions: regret + gerúndio vs infinitivo, make/do/take a decision, back out of vs turn down;
- 1 miniDialogues (Lena + Tom, 5 linhas);
- 2 productionTasks.
